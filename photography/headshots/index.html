Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a session take?

Each session takes approximately 30 minutes, including setup and brief consultation.

What should I wear?

We recommend solid colors that complement your skin tone. Avoid busy patterns and logos. Bring 2-3 outfit options for variety.

When will I receive my photos?

You'll receive your edited photos within 5-7 business days after your session.

Can I use these photos for my business?

Yes! All packages include a print release, allowing you to use the photos for both personal and professional purposes.
