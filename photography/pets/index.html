Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to the photo session?

Bring your pet's favorite treats, toys, and any accessories you'd like to include in the photos. You might want to bring a brush for quick touch-ups. Most importantly, bring patience and a positive attitude!

How do you handle anxious or energetic pets?

We take a patient, relaxed approach and let your pet set the pace. We'll take breaks as needed and use positive reinforcement. For high-energy pets, we might start with action shots to help them burn off energy before moving to posed photos.

How long does a typical session last?

Most sessions last between 1 hour. This gives us plenty of time to let your pet get comfortable and capture their true personality.

Is there a travel fee?

Travel within 20 miles of Vancouver downtown is included. Locations beyond that are subject to a $1/mile travel fee.

Can my pet stay on a leash?

Absolutely, and I actually recommend it. Leashes can be edited out during post-processing if you prefer.

Can I be in the photos too?

Yes, absolutely! You're welcome to be part of the session with your pet. Whether it's a sweet cuddle, a walk together, or a playful moment—these are often the most meaningful shots.
