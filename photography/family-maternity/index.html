Common Questions

Everything you need to know about your photography session.

When is the best time for a maternity session?

The ideal time is between 28-34 weeks of pregnancy when your bump is beautifully round but you're still comfortable. We recommend booking your session in advance to secure your preferred date.

How old should my baby be for a newborn session?

The best time is within the first 5-14 days after birth when babies are sleepier and more flexible for those curled-up poses. However, we can capture beautiful images at any age!

What should we wear for a family session?

We recommend coordinating colors rather than matching outfits. Soft, neutral tones photograph beautifully. We offer a complimentary wardrobe consultation to help you prepare.

Can you come to our home for sessions?

Absolutely! In-home sessions are perfect for newborn and family photography. Your home provides a comfortable, personal setting that creates meaningful images.

How long until we receive our photos?

Your private online gallery will be ready within 2-3 weeks after your session. You'll receive a link to view, download, and share your images.

Do you offer print products?

Yes! We offer beautiful albums, canvas prints, and fine art prints. There's nothing quite like holding your memories in your hands.
